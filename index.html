How to play

The clock

The big button changes as the round goes: GO! starts the 30-second clock and music, Stop freezes it, and Clear board starts a fresh round. When time runs out you'll see Time's up!, and anything you check after that is marked late.

Letters

Pick Vowel or Consonant until the board is full, then find the longest word you can. Classic deals 9 letters; Street Countdown deals 18.

Type words into Check a word to see if they're in the dictionary and can be made from the board. Your words stay listed, with your best highlighted. Answers shows the longest possible words and underlines any you found.

Numbers

Choose how many big numbers (25, 50, 75, 100) you want, from 0 to 4, then Generate target. Use + − × ÷ to get as close as you can. Every step must give a whole number above zero, and each number can be used once.

Type your working into Your method, e.g. (75 + 3) * 6, to check it and see your points: 10 for exact, 7 within 5, 5 within 10. Solution says whether it can be done exactly, then Next step reveals the working one line at a time.

Conundrum

New conundrum shows a scrambled 9-letter word and starts the clock straight away. Type your guess; a correct answer shows how long you took. Reveal gives up.

Options

Style
Studio Blue, Quiz Sheet or Amber LED. Quiz Sheet is easiest to read on a TV in a bright room.
Sound
Turn the clock music on or off. The clock still runs.
Auto-start
Starts the clock by itself after a 3 or 5 second "get ready" pause, once the letters are dealt. In the numbers round the target comes up as soon as you choose the numbers, then the pause starts.
Answers at time's up
Shows the answers, solution or conundrum as soon as the clock runs out.
Piles carry over
Like the show: letters already dealt stay out of the piles in later rounds, until the piles are reshuffled.
Repeat cap
No more than 2 of the same consonant on a board (3 in Street Countdown).

Options are remembered in this browser. At Christmas the site dresses up by itself.
